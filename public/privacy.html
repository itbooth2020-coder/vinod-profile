Who runs this site

This is the personal profile site of Vinod Pyarelal Yadav (www.vinodyadav.com). Contact: yadav.vinod579@gmail.com.

What the site collects

The site has no accounts, forms, analytics, advertising or tracking, and sets no cookies of its own.

Your browser's own storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) keeps a few preferences on your device only: light or dark theme, colour palette and font, whether animations are paused, whether VINCE's voice is muted, and whether VINCE's welcome hint has been shown. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Hosting

The site is served by Amazon Web Services (Amazon S3 and CloudFront). As with any website, delivering a page means the server receives your IP address, the page requested and your browser's user agent. If CloudFront access logging is turned on, these request details are stored in logs used only to run and secure the site.

Third parties your browser may contact

  • Google Fonts: only if you pick Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Sans or Source Serif in the Customize panel (that choice is remembered, so later visits load it too). Google then receives your IP address and browser details. The default font is hosted on this site. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Your browser's speech services: VINCE, the voice assistant, speaks with your browser's built-in speech synthesis and, if you press the microphone button, listens with your browser's speech recognition. Depending on your browser and the voice it uses, the browser vendor (for example Google, Microsoft or Apple) may process that text or audio on its servers. Nothing is listened to unless you press the microphone button.

Company logos, the profile photo and the Blogs section's latest posts are files on this site. The post titles, summaries and images are copied from Blogger (Google) when the site is built, so no third-party service, Blogger and Google included, is contacted to show them. Blogger only sees your visit if you open a post or a blog.

Role fit, the prompt console and VINCE

The role-fit check runs entirely in your browser: the job description you paste never leaves your device. On this site the prompt console and VINCE also answer offline, by searching the resume data built into the page; your questions are not sent to any server or AI service. If a live AI assistant is added in the future, this notice will be updated first to say what is sent and to whom.

External links

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Facebook, X, the blogs and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Your rights and contact
